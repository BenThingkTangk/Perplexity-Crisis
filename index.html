Crisis thesis

The problem is structural, not incidental.

Perplexity's stack has evolved into four parallel compute planes with no shared abstraction, no unified support owner, and expensive traffic bouncing between clouds. Each provider deal is rational. The composite is fragile — and every incident becomes a multi-vendor blame spiral, exactly as users demand sub-second answers.

Insertion strategy

Akamai is the distribution brain, not the new religion.

Leave training and heavyweight fine-tuning where they already run. Insert Akamai's AI Grid between AI factories and the end user. Surgical, not theological — and directly attacks latency variance, routing incoherence, and support fragmentation.

Without convergence

  • Queries bounce across centralized origins — peak-hour TTFT degrades
  • Cross-cloud egress quietly compounds cost as scale grows
  • Support tickets straddle three or four competing vendors
  • International routing and compliance multiply edge cases
  • Comet-style agent disputes (e.g. Amazon court order) leak into UX

With Akamai as inference distribution

  • User requests route to the nearest of 4,400+ POPs by AI Grid policy
  • Semantic caching trims repeat work on the same token surface
  • Workload-aware control plane: cost-per-token, TTFT, throughput
  • One additional vendor owns the user-facing distribution layer
  • Region-aware routing keeps compliance traffic geographically honest
Akamai is not there to replace AWS, Foundry, or CoreWeave.
It is there to make their existing strategy behave like one system.
